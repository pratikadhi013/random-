steps to make a tea

  1. Boil Water and Spices: Pour the water into a small saucepan or pot and turn the heat to medium-high. If using ginger or cardamom, crush them slightly with a heavy object and add them to the water. Let the water come to a rolling boil.
  2. Add Tea Leaves: Add the tea leaves or granules and the sugar to the boiling water. Let it boil for about 2 to 3 minutes so the water absorbs the color and flavor of the tea.
  3. Pour in the Milk: Pour the milk into the boiling mixture. The liquid will change to a lighter, milky shade.
  4. Simmer the Tea: Bring the mixture to a complete boil. Watch closely so it does not overflow. Reduce the flame to low and let it simmer for another 2 to 3 minutes until it turns a rich brownish color.
  5. Strain and Serve: Turn off the stove. Place a fine-mesh tea strainer over your cup or glass, and carefully pour the hot tea through it to catch the loose leaves and spices. Enjoy your hot chai!
